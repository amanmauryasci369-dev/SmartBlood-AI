## 10 Key Differentiators from e-RaktKosh
1. **Predictive Foreknowledge vs Static Listing**: Forecasts shortages 7 to 30 days before they occur.
2. **Four-Tier Availability Taxonomy**: Strictly avoids misleading patients by distinguishing `Reported` vs `Confirmed` stock.
3. **Proactive Spoilage Balancing**: Transfers near-expiry units to high-volume trauma centers before expiration.
4. **Emergency Corridors**: Multi-criteria spatial routing calculating traffic transit time and cold chain compliance.
5. **Donor Matching with Privacy**: Ranks donors without publicly leaking personal phone numbers.
6. **Smart Anomaly Alerts**: Flags unexpected inventory drops or epidemic surges.
7. **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Provides medical officers with clear justifications for every recommendation.
8. **Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Boundaries**: Preserves physician authority by never auto-issuing units.
9. **Role-Tailored Dashboards**: Custom interfaces for Admins, Blood Banks, Hospitals, Donors, and Patients.
10. **Extensible Adapter Architecture**: Plug-and-play adapter design for future authorized API gateways.
