# AI Integration: Predictive Models, Explainability & Decision Support

## 1. Unified Intelligence Framework
Rather than maintaining disconnected prediction tools, SmartBlood AI connects its machine learning components directly to live inventory and logistics workflows:

1. **Demand Forecasting (`app/ml/demand_forecast.py`)**:
   - Model: Scikit-Learn `RandomForestRegressor`
   - Features: Day of week, month, component type, blood group, trauma center flag, hospital bed capacity, dengue seasonal index.
   - Genuine empirical test metrics: $\text{MAE} = 7.73$, $\text{RMSE} = 9.71$, $R^2 = 0.342$.
   - Multi-horizon projections: 1-day, 3-day, 7-day, 30-day.

2. **FEFO Shelf-Life Classification (`app/ml/expiry_risk.py`)**:
   - Model: Scikit-Learn `RandomForestClassifier`
   - Triages inventory into `LOW_RISK`, `MEDIUM_RISK`, `HIGH_RISK` based on days to expiry, component fragility, and storage temperature compliance.

3. **Baseline Spoilage Risk Model (`app/ml/wastage_predictor.py`)**:
   - Deterministic multi-factor scoring evaluating current inventory, shelf-life velocity, and local demand absorption.
   - Labeled transparently as "Baseline Risk Model" (Zero invented accuracy).

4. **Multi-Factor Quantitative Shortage Balance Equation**:
   $$\text{Projected Stock} = \text{Current Stock} + \text{Incoming Supply} - \text{Predicted Demand} - \text{Expected Expiry Loss}$$

5. **Multi-Factor Donor Matching**:
   $$\text{Match Score} = 0.30 \cdot \text{Compatibility} + 0.25 \cdot \text{Eligibility} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Proximity} + 0.25 \cdot \text{Response Prob}$$

## 2. Explainability & Medical Safety Guardrails
Every recommendation generated by the AI includes:
- **Plain-English Clinical Explainability**: Explains *why* the provider was selected (e.g., proximity, confirmed cold-chain, impending expiry).
- **Non-Coercive CDS Notice**:
  > "Clinical Decision Support Only. Verification and physical cross-match certification by authorized clinical personnel is mandatory prior to blood unit issuance or transfusion."
